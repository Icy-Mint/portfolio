What I Do

My background blends engineering, data, and software. I started as an analytics engineer, spending years translating messy, cross-functional data problems into usable systems — which pulled me toward full-stack and AI engineering. Today I build end-to-end products, applied ML systems, agentic workflows, and developer-facing tools. My engineering background also adds domain depth in energy, mechanical engineering, and sustainability.

Full-Stack Development

Full-stack web apps shipped end-to-end — from rapid prototypes on Vercel and Render to production systems on AWS with distributed architecture, authentication, and infrastructure planning.

Applied Machine Learning

Fine-tuning and evaluating LLMs and deep learning models in PyTorch, building agentic workflows, and applying NLP and computer vision to domain-specific technical tasks.

Simulation & Systems Engineering

CFD modeling of airflow, aerosol transport, and infection-risk / thermal comfort (RANS, multiphase turbulence), alongside building energy simulation in EnergyPlus, IES-VE, and eQuest.

Energy & Sustainability (domain experience)

Energy data analysis, embodied carbon & lifecycle assessment, preliminary renewable energy sizing, decarbonization planning, and weather/climate analysis for buildings and hardware.
