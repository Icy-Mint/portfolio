What I Do

My background blends engineering, data, and software. I started as an analytics engineer, spending years working directly with clients and cross-functional teams to translate messy, real-world problems into usable systems — which pulled me toward full-stack and AI engineering. Today I build end-to-end products, applied ML systems, agentic workflows, and developer-facing tools. My engineering background also adds domain depth in energy, mechanical engineering, and sustainability.
